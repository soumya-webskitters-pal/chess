Your data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Local game data

Quantum Chess stores game preferences and completed-game history in your browser using local storage. This information remains on your device unless you clear the site’s browser data.

Online matches

Live friend matches use WebRTC through PeerJS signaling. Moves, room codes, reactions, and messages travel between the connected players as needed to operate the current match. PeerJS may process technical connection metadata required to establish the session.

Messages and reactions

Chat messages and reactions are sent to the other player and are not added to your saved match history. Do not share passwords, financial details, addresses, or other sensitive personal information in game chat.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

Quantum Chess does not require a user account. The current version does not include advertising or behavioral analytics and does not maintain a creator-operated player database.

Your choices

You can remove locally stored preferences and history by clearing this site’s browser data. You can end an online connection at any time by selecting Exit during a live match.

Children and fair use

The game is intended as general entertainment. Players should not use online messages to request personal information from children or other players.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the creator, Soumya Pal.
